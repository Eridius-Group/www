What we do.

Advocate for your rights

We promote the right to privacy everywhere we can. We don't just claim to care about your privacy - we're ready to defend it. We are prepared to spread the word far and wide that the average consumer, who is aware of their privacy being violated, is not okay with it.

We hold online and in-person classes for computer literacy, privacy and security baseline standards, and privacy rights - educating America's youth and elderly alike on what to look out for, so they aren't taken advantage of by scammers and malicious software.

Provide public recommendations

We will recommend products to consumers based on our standards and ettiquete, frequently partnering with providers to give you deals and promotions. We only recommend software we wholly trust and use personally - not just whoever pays the highest dollar.

Codychat Chat Utilities

Codychat Chat Utilities (CCUtils) is a dedicated browser extension designed to help reduce offensive, inappropriate, and unwanted content in Codychat online chatrooms.

By offering a robust suite of customizable settings, tweaks, and content filters, CCUtils streamlines the chat experience. It provides quicker, more efficient ways to manage platform features and handle various types of content, empowering both moderators and everyday users to take control of their environment.
